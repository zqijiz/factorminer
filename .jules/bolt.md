## 2024-05-24 - Pandas Ranking Outperforms SciPy for Cross-Sectional Data
**Learning:** In cross-sectional financial time-series metrics (like IC, pairwise correlation, and ranking), `scipy.stats.rankdata` introduces severe overhead and defaults to `nan_policy='propagate'` (requiring pre-filtering via loops) or `nan_policy='omit'` (which is extremely slow on large 2D arrays). By contrast, `pd.DataFrame(x).rank(axis=0, method='average', na_option='keep')` is fully vectorized and ~2.5x to 3x faster than loop-based `rankdata` for 2D arrays, correctly preserving tie semantics and missing value gaps. Even for 1D arrays inside loops, `pd.Series(x).rank(method='average', na_option='keep').to_numpy()` proved faster than `rankdata(x)`.
**Action:** When performing cross-sectional rank-based operations (like Spearman correlation or quantile assignment) on large matrices with NaNs and ties, avoid `scipy.stats.rankdata` in time-loops. Instead, cast to a pandas DataFrame/Series, use its `.rank(na_option='keep')` method, and extract `.to_numpy()`.
