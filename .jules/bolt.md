## 2024-06-08 - Vectorizing batched Spearman correlations over time axis
**Learning:** In `factorminer/evaluation/correlation.py`, computing time-averaged cross-sectional correlations over loops (like `for t in range(T)`) was slow when executed frequently for library replacement testing. By replacing these loops with vectorized aggregations across `axis=0` utilizing memory-safe mask patterns (e.g. `np.where(valid, ...)` and `np.divide(where=mask)`), execution speed improved significantly. However, optimizing rank extraction `scipy.stats.rankdata` must preserve exact `method='average'` tie-handling to guarantee correctness.
**Action:** When vectorizing time-averaged metrics over large 2D slices, pre-calculate `valid = ~(np.isnan(a) | np.isnan(b))` blocks. Use branchless `np.where` masking, perform `sum(axis=0)` operations, and apply safe division arrays via `np.divide(out=..., where=...)`. Avoid overriding exact `rankdata` behavior.
