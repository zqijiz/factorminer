## 2026-05-30 - Optimize KAMA by moving volatility pre-calculation outside time loop
**Learning:** Sequential time series algorithms (like EMA and KAMA) require O(T) sequential processing where element t depends on element t-1, making full vectorization impossible. However, intermediate aggregations (like cumulative sum or volatility calculations) that are repeatedly performed inside the loop but don't strictly depend on the sequential state can be pre-calculated. In KAMA, calculating `diff` and `nansum` inside the loop creates an O(T^2) complexity bottleneck.
**Action:** Vectorize volatility and scaling constants entirely outside the time loop using `np.cumsum` / `torch.cumsum` and shift operations. Then, use branchless assignment `np.where` / `torch.where` inside the tight loop to significantly speed up KAMA calculations (e.g. from ~5.2s to ~0.97s on PyTorch). Keep operations outside the tight loop as much as possible.
