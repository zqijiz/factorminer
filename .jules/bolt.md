## 2024-06-24 - [Performance Pattern: Vectorized Cumsum & Branchless Assignments in Sequential Loops]
**Learning:** In sequential time series algorithms (like EMA and KAMA) where element t depends on element t-1, we can't fully vectorize the time loop. However, replacing boolean slice indexing assignments (e.g., `out[valid, t] = ...`) inside the loop with branchless `np.where(valid, ..., out[:, t])` avoids creating expensive intermediate arrays and speeds up the tight loop by ~10-20%. Furthermore, extracting operations like rolling volatility from inside the loop by pre-calculating it outside with `np.cumsum` padded by zeros significantly speeds up operations (e.g. KAMA speedup by ~40%).
**Action:** Always extract aggregations out of time loops with `cumsum` or sliding window views. Inside tight time loops, always prefer branchless `np.where`/`torch.where` assignments instead of boolean slice assignment over views to reduce overhead.
